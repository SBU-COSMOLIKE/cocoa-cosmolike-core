# What the two upstream repositories actually compute

> **Archive status.** This source study records design proposals and validation requirements, not completed features. Read the [maintained implementation status](../threepcf.md) first. Both the mean and its covariance belong in `cosmolike/3pcf/`; earlier destination proposals using `fastnc/` or `covariances/` are superseded.

This chapter studies the source that a CosmoLike implementation would inherit. The strongest opportunity is to separate triangle geometry, cosmology, intrinsic-alignment amplitudes, and source-bin projection into calculations with different lifetimes. Several such separations already exist in `fastnc`. Others are absent from the TATT path or are repeated by the surrounding CosmoSIS interface. These observations justify a measured implementation project; they do not establish a numerical speedup.

The inspected `fastnc` revision is [`7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce`](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/tree/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce), the public `tatt` branch retrieved for this study. The inspected `3pcf_integrator` revision is [`d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944`](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/tree/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944). The latter inspection covers selected source and configuration files, with the full repository tree used to establish file availability. Neither repository was installed or executed. Findings below distinguish behavior visible in the source from numerical consequences that require tests.

## 1. The physical calculation and its representation in the source

### A triangle carries field labels as well as side lengths

The matter bispectrum describes how three Fourier modes correlate. Their wavevectors close into a triangle: their vector sum is zero. For a matter-only bispectrum, exchanging two sides and their associated matter fields leaves the same statistical object. Intrinsic alignments introduce a distinction between the matter-density field, denoted here by $`\delta`$, and the intrinsic ellipticity E mode, denoted by $`E`$. $`B_{\delta\delta E}(k_1,k_2,k_3)`$ places the intrinsic shape on the third Fourier leg. Moving that shape to the first leg requires permuting both the arguments and the field labels.

The TATT implementation follows this organization explicitly. Its low-level calculator forms $`B_{\delta\delta E}`$, $`B_{\delta E\delta}`$, $`B_{E\delta\delta}`$, the three arrangements with two E fields, and $`B_{EEE}`$. The projection code then assigns a lensing efficiency to a matter leg and a source-distribution weight to an intrinsic-shape leg. For example, the `ddE` contribution uses the first two source bins' lensing efficiencies and the third bin's intrinsic-shape weight. A C implementation must retain this association even if it computes several permutations together. [IA component construction](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/ia_bispectra.py#L624), [projection weights](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L807).

### The projection is a sum of shared physics times bin-dependent weights

Let $`t`$ identify a Fourier triangle, $`r`$ a radial integration node, $`q`$ a field arrangement such as `ddE`, and $`i,j,k`$ the source bins. The discrete calculation has the form

```math
B^{ijk}_{q}(t)=\sum_r w_r\,K^{ijk}_{q}(r)\,
 B_q\!\left(\frac{\ell_1(t)}{\chi_r},
             \frac{\ell_2(t)}{\chi_r},
             \frac{\ell_3(t)}{\chi_r},z_r\right).
```

Here $`w_r`$ is the radial quadrature weight, $`\chi_r`$ is comoving distance, and $`K^{ijk}_{q}`$ contains the source weights and geometric factors in the chosen convention. At fixed cosmology and global IA parameters, the final factor is independent of the bin triple. The expensive triangle physics can therefore be evaluated once, followed by several weighted sums. This remains true for distinct source distributions; the distributions change the weights. Per-bin IA amplitudes would require a further separation into amplitude-independent terms rather than reusing one already-combined IA spectrum.

The source uses distances in Mpc/$`h`$ and wavenumbers in $`h`$/Mpc. Its stored lensing kernel omits factors that another convention might place inside the kernel: the common projection factor is $`(1+z)^3/\chi`$. Its intrinsic-shape weight includes $`a/\chi`$ to make that convention consistent. These factors should be derived once when defining the C interface, with dimensions checked at every stage. Copying a CosmoLike kernel and keeping the upstream final prefactor without reconciling the definitions would count some distance or scale-factor powers twice. [Distance construction](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L339), [kernel definitions](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L416).

### The angular calculation replaces repeated oscillatory integrals

After projection, `fastnc` decomposes the bispectrum's dependence on triangle opening angle into multipoles. A multipole is a coefficient multiplying a known angular basis function; it separates the angle dependence from the two remaining triangle coordinates. `multipole.py` precomputes the required basis integrals. The method named `gauss-legendre` evaluates integrals of a piecewise-linear interpolant using these basis integrals and tensor contractions. A replacement must reproduce that discretization before changing the quadrature, because a different numerical integral is a separate accuracy decision. [Multipole decomposition](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/multipole.py#L52).

The next stage contracts bispectrum multipoles $`b_L`$ with geometric coupling functions $`G_{LM}`$ to obtain a two-dimensional function $`H_M(\ell_1,\ell_2)`$. The labels $`L`$ and $`M`$ refer to angular expansion orders; neither is the physical size of a triangle. Two-dimensional FFTLog then transforms $`H_M`$ into the natural-component multipoles of the shear three-point function. FFTLog expresses a function sampled uniformly in logarithmic coordinates in a basis suitable for Bessel transforms. Its speed comes from using Fourier transforms and analytic Bessel-transform factors, rather than separately integrating every requested pair of angular separations. [Coupling contraction](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/fastnc.py#L231), [two-Bessel implementation](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/twobessel.py#L159).

The natural components are four complex shear correlations distinguished by which shear factors are complex-conjugated. Complex numbers preserve the orientation information of a spin-two field. `FastNaturalComponents.compute` reconstructs their opening-angle dependence, changes the shear-projection convention, and returns arrays indexed by two angular separations and opening angle. The CosmoSIS interface then applies an overall minus sign and exchanges components 2 and 3 before passing them to the aperture-mass calculation. That exchange is part of the observable convention, not an optional arrangement of memory. [Natural-component calculation](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/fastnc.py#L319), [TreeCorr convention conversion](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_interface.py#L229).

### The final aperture statistic is a linear operation on those components

`nc_to_map3.py` builds the triangle grid, converts angular units from radians to arcminutes, and calls `fast_map3.calculateMap3`. That function calculates complex aperture-filter weights and contracts them with the four natural components. At fixed angular bins and aperture radii, this is a fixed linear transformation. Its weights depend on geometry, bin widths, and the aperture definition, but not on cosmology. The source already performs the final contractions through NumPy matrix products; replacing those products with a scalar C loop would not itself be an optimization. [Aperture interface](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/nc_to_map3.py#L12), [filter contraction](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fast_map3.py#L118).

## 2. Optimizations already present upstream

`fastnc` already avoids several expensive repetitions. The matter branch of `BispectrumBase.interpolate` passes the computed matter bispectrum from one source-bin triple to the next through `bm`. Only the line-of-sight weighting changes. Describing the upstream matter path as recomputing the entire matter bispectrum for every tomographic triple would be incorrect. The reuse assumes that the triangles, radial grid, cosmology, and baryon prescription are common. [Matter-bispectrum reuse](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L945).

The geometric coupling calculation has a disk cache, can extend a cache with missing multipoles, and avoids interpolating repeatedly at duplicate angular ratios within one call. There is also a `use_GLM_table` option for tabulating the coupling on the actual FFT grid. A benchmark must distinguish constructing this geometry with an empty cache from evaluating a new cosmology after the geometry has been prepared. Those are different workloads. [Coupling cache and initialization](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/coupling.py#L113), [FFT-grid coupling table](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/coupling.py#L195).

The forward two-dimensional FFT is already shared across the four natural components for a given $`M`$. `compute` creates one `two_Bessel` object outside the component loop. Its constructor computes the Fourier coefficients once; each component then changes the Bessel orders and performs its inverse transform. A proposal to move the forward FFT outside that component loop would repeat an existing optimization. Additional reuse is possible in the Bessel factors, coordinate powers, buffers, and FFT plans, but that is a different claim. [Loop ordering](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/fastnc.py#L354), [forward transform in initialization](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/twobessel.py#L17).

NumPy already executes the array arithmetic, Fourier transforms, and matrix products in compiled libraries. The Python-level costs include constructing many intermediate arrays, constructing interpolators, orchestrating repeated operations, and retaining more data than the final statistic requires. This is why the proposed C calculation should be judged against the actual compiled operations beneath NumPy, with matched thread counts. Counting Python lines gives no estimate of runtime.

The integrator also contains an emulator path that bypasses the explicit natural-component calculation. In the inspected `blind-metacal-796sims-february-Percival` configuration, `fastnc` and `nc_to_map3` are commented out and `fastnc_emulate` is active. A C implementation of the explicit model should first be compared with the explicit model. Comparing it with the emulator answers a separate question involving training cost, validity range, interpolation errors, and parameter derivatives. [Inspected inference configuration](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/config/blind-metacal-796sims-february-Percival/blind-metacal-shear-2pt-map3-NLA-all.ini#L14).

## 3. Source-supported opportunities for reducing work

### Reuse TATT triangle calculations across tomographic triples

The TATT branch of `interpolate` calls `kappa_bispectrum_IA_direct` separately for every requested sample combination without passing a previous `ia_bispec_comps` result. That projection routine already accepts such a result, but the inspected caller does not use the reuse mechanism. The seven projected E-mode terms therefore start from repeated evaluations of the same three-dimensional IA physics when the IA parameters are global. This is a stronger reason for changing the organization than the choice of programming language. [TATT interpolation loop](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L927), [optional IA reuse argument](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L762).

The expected structure is a tile of triangles and radial nodes, followed by projections into several bin triples while that tile remains in memory. A tile is a bounded group of neighboring array entries. It allows a thread to use the same physics values several times without retaining the full triangle-by-redshift array for every component. The projection weights can be prepared as rows, with one row per bin triple and IA arrangement. This also makes the approximation visible: any restriction on which bins can correlate enters the weights or output list, rather than being hidden in a numerical helper.

The memory cost is already large enough to influence this choice. The TATT perimeter grid has 35 default scale values and 40 values on each of its two fractional-side axes. With the interface's 170 radial nodes, one full double-precision triangle-by-redshift array has 9,520,000 entries, or 76.16 MB in decimal units. Seven such component arrays occupy 533.12 MB; nineteen occupy 1.44704 GB before temporary geometry, power spectra, projection weights, and copies. These figures follow from the array dimensions, not from a memory measurement. Actual peak usage must be measured. Giving each worker a full copy would multiply this cost. [TATT grid](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L211), [radial grid construction](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/utils.py#L40), [interface radial settings](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_interface.py#L30).

### Share geometry among IA terms and request only needed outputs

`get_ia_bispectra` constructs nineteen E/B combinations. `BispectrumTATT.ia_bispectrum` subsequently keeps seven E-only combinations. Several lower-level functions reconstruct triangle vectors and dot products for the same shapes and permutations. An E-only aperture calculation can avoid evaluating unused B-containing components, provided that the retained outputs are checked against the full calculation. A separate all-component request is needed for reproducing results involving those components or testing parity. Removing unused outputs from one request must not silently remove physical capability from the library. [Full component calculation](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/ia_bispectra.py#L597), [E-only wrapper](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L1313).

Geometry should be stored as explicitly named scalar products, squared side lengths, and permutation indices. That would allow the C expressions to remain close to the equations while avoiding repeated vector construction. The amplitudes $`C_1`$, $`C_{1\delta}`$, $`C_2`$, and the velocity-shear coefficient depend on redshift and nuisance parameters. Computing them once per radial node separates their physical evolution from triangle shape. This is also the natural starting point for analytic derivatives with respect to amplitudes: the derivative acts on an explicit coefficient rather than on an opaque whole prediction.

### Prepare the transforms and aperture filters once per geometry

Inside the integrator's sample-combination loop, `nc.set_bispectrum(bs)` calls `set_fftgrid` every time. That rebuilds the coordinate meshes and, if requested, the coupling table even when only the tomographic triple changes. `kappa_bispectrum_multipole` also constructs a regular-grid interpolator separately for each multipole. `two_Bessel` reconstructs powers and complex gamma-function factors for every transformed output. These objects have different dependencies: interpolation values change with cosmology, whereas interpolation brackets, coordinate powers, and Bessel factors can remain fixed when the grids remain fixed. [Repeated grid setup](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_interface.py#L222), [multipole interpolation](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L1053).

The aperture weights are recalculated for each call to `calculateMap3`. A prepared operator can include these weights, the quadrature factors, the component convention conversion, and the final real-part combination. If only aperture masses are required, opening-angle reconstruction and aperture integration can also be combined algebraically. However, that combined operation must use the same finite triangle range, binning, and cuts as the reference. The continuous aperture-mass integral is not automatically the same statistic as a discretized measurement missing some triangle configurations.

FFT lengths also need care. `get_tuned_fftgrid` changes the logarithmic Fourier grid so the requested angular bins fall on its output samples. The configured `nfft` is a nominal minimum, not always the final transform length; the TATT branch's odd-length adjustment can even round below that request at a boundary. Selecting a nearby FFT-friendly size is admissible only after preserving or rederiving the angular alignment. Changing the transform length while retaining obsolete downsampling indices would change the angular positions being evaluated. [Grid tuning](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/fastnc.py#L786), [boundary cases](13_second_pass_numerics.md).

## 4. TATT and velocity-shear choices that must remain explicit

The source converts the input nuisance parameters into coefficients according to

```math
C_1(z)=-A_1\,c_1\rho_{\rm crit}\,\Omega_m
       \frac{[(1+z)/(1+z_p)]^{\alpha_1}}{D(z)},
\qquad C_{1\delta}(z)=b_{\rm TA}C_1(z),
```

```math
C_2(z)=5 A_2\,c_1\rho_{\rm crit}\,\Omega_m
       \frac{[(1+z)/(1+z_p)]^{\alpha_2}}{D^2(z)}.
```

Here $`D(z)`$ is the supplied growth factor, $`z_p`$ is the pivot redshift, and the dimensionless normalization $`c_1\rho_{\rm crit}`$ is 0.0134 in the implementation. The normalization of the supplied growth factor must agree with the convention used to define $`A_1`$ and $`A_2`$. The variable called `Ct` on input is then multiplied by $`C_1(z)`$ inside the calculator. Thus the input is a ratio-like amplitude in this path, rather than directly the physical coefficient appearing in every equation. A Fisher derivative with respect to that input has a different meaning from a derivative with respect to an independent dimensional or redshift-dependent coefficient. [Coefficient definitions](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/ia_bispectra.py#L597).

The default `do_non_linear=True` replaces the power spectra used in the IA expressions with the nonlinear-input reader. That reader receives the first redshift row from the CosmoSIS nonlinear-power table and scales it by $`D^2(z)`$. This is a stated source behavior, not a full nonlinear $`P(k,z)`$ calculation: nonlinear growth generally changes shape with redshift. Replacing it with CosmoLike's redshift-dependent nonlinear power would change the model as well as its implementation. Reproduction and improved modeling need separate results, including an account of what power-spectrum prescription the second paper used. [Power input and evolution](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/ia_bispectra.py#L106), [interface input row](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_interface.py#L159).

The option `remove_alignment` subtracts $`C_1 B_{\rm tree}`$, $`C_1^2 B_{\rm tree}`$, or $`C_1^3 B_{\rm tree}`$ from the corresponding E-mode terms. This can define a residual intended to accompany another alignment model. Whether it avoids double counting depends on exactly which baseline is added later and whether that baseline uses the same matter bispectrum. A BiHalofit baseline and a tree-shaped expression evaluated using nonlinear powers are different functions. The model definition must say which part is retained from each. [Subtraction terms](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/ia_bispectra.py#L743).

The emulator uses one cosmological network and seven IA-component networks, predicts their aperture contributions on redshift grids, and performs the source-kernel weighting afterwards. This is evidence that radial separation is useful beyond the explicit FFT calculation. It also means the emulator's training targets are part of the model definition. Their normalization, subtraction convention, pixel window, triangle cuts, and parameter ordering cannot be recovered reliably from the network filenames alone. [Emulator setup](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_emulate_tatt.py#L94), [weighted assembly](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_emulate_tatt.py#L327).

## 5. Specific checks required before adopting this source as the reference

### Independent $`A_2`$ and $`\alpha_2`$ inputs

The direct interface sets `alphaIA_2` from the data-block entry `a2`, rather than `alpha2`. That value is passed through `IA_params` into the exponent in $`C_2(z)`$. The current source therefore ties two physically distinct parameters in this path. Test this by fixing a nonzero $`A_2`$, varying only $`\alpha_2`$, and evaluating the unprojected coefficients and spectra at several redshifts away from the pivot. Then vary $`A_2`$ alone. The network-input vector reads `alpha2` separately, so direct and emulated predictions require a matched parameter-injection test. The corresponding mistaken assignment also appears in the emulator's auxiliary bispectrum object, although that object's setter alone does not establish the effect on the network prediction. [Direct assignment](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_interface.py#L152), [network inputs](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_emulate_tatt.py#L215).

### The two IA formula paths are not two verified references

The source contains `get_B_ddE_gomes`, `get_B_dEE_gomes`, and `get_B_EEE_gomes`, with equation references and optional velocity-shear terms. The normal calculation sets `direct_methods=False`. The alternate methods call helpers such as `_B_ddd` and `_khat_xy` that are not defined in this pinned class. The dormant branch also omits `Ct` when invoking those methods, and its assignment of two single-E permutations differs from the active branch. These facts prevent treating the dormant branch as an independent numerical verification. The necessary comparison is an independently specified, runnable equation evaluation with the same coordinate frame, field ordering, power inputs, and nonzero velocity-shear coefficient. [Alternate formulas](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/ia_bispectra.py#L400), [inactive dispatch](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/ia_bispectra.py#L729).

### Side-exchange symmetry with mixed fields and bins

`kappa_bispectrum_multipole` folds angular ratios above $`\pi/4`$ into the other half of its domain. `FastNaturalComponents.compute` reconstructs negative angular modes using transposes. These reductions need the relevant side-exchange identities. A matter-only bispectrum has the required symmetry, but a selected IA term or a fixed mixed-bin triple need not be symmetric under exchanging lengths while leaving its labels fixed. Compare all ordered permutations for an asymmetric triangle and three different source bins, carrying the field and bin labels with every permutation. Compare the folded calculation with a full-domain reference. A failure would require changing the symmetry reduction, rather than merely increasing the grid resolution. [Multipole folding](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L1071), [negative-mode reconstruction](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/fastnc.py#L382).

### Physical triangles and nonfinite values

The rectangular TATT perimeter grid includes points outside the triangle domain: for example, two small fractional sides can leave the third side larger than their sum. Vector construction clips a cosine into its allowed interval, and the higher-level wrapper replaces NaN and infinite component values with zero. These operations can hide the distinction between a deliberately excluded unphysical cell and a failed evaluation of a physical triangle. Record a physical-domain mask, count nonfinite values before replacement, and verify interpolation near the domain boundary against direct physical-triangle evaluations. Interpolation stencils must not mix artificial zeros into a physical result without a justified boundary prescription. [Perimeter grid](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L211), [vector construction](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/ia_bispectra.py#L146), [replacement of nonfinite values](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L1324).

### The FFT taper is calculated but not returned

`two_sph_bessel.get_c_mn` constructs `c_mn_filter` using the configured frequency window, then returns `c_mn`. The returned coefficients are therefore untapered in this path. This is a direct source discrepancy between the smoothing description and the returned array. It does not show that the reported observables are inaccurate. Compare transform and derivative convergence with the returned coefficients and with the intended taper, using analytic inputs and representative physical spectra. Enabling the taper during a C port would be a numerical-method change and must not be included silently in a performance comparison. [Returned coefficients](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/twobessel.py#L73).

### Unequal aperture radii require their own test

`fast_map3.py` uses `k2.all() == 1` and `k3.all() == 1` to choose its equal-radius expressions and orientation-counting rule. Array `.all()` asks whether every element is nonzero; it does not ask whether every element equals one. Positive unequal radius ratios satisfy this condition. Equal-radius configurations do not expose the discrepancy. Before forecasting generalized aperture combinations, compare deliberately unequal positive radii with an independent filter integral and verify the orientation multiplicities. [Filter condition](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fast_map3.py#L39), [counting condition](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fast_map3.py#L152).

### Isolated objects must retain independent settings

Several configuration dictionaries are defined at class scope, and `update_config` mutates the supplied dictionary in place. In the inspected subclasses, `config_IA` is inherited rather than copied. The TATT emulator constructs a matter object with `NLA=True` followed by a TATT object with `NLA=False`; these objects therefore share the inherited dictionary unless another layer intervenes. An object-order test should create each object alone and then create the pair in both orders, checking settings and predictions. This also means that the initial configuration labels alone cannot establish whether the executed emulator sums an NLA baseline and an IA residual. The active state has to be inspected. [Shared dictionaries](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L67), [in-place update](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/utils.py#L117), [paired construction](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_emulate_tatt.py#L99).

### Emulator coordinates and shell normalization need explicit records

For a cosmological-model option other than `wCDM`, the TATT emulator appends 0.0 in the input position named `w`. A cosmological constant corresponds to $`w=-1`$. Determine whether the training convention intended a transformed coordinate; if it used the physical equation-of-state parameter, compare the LambdaCDM path with the wCDM path evaluated at $`w=-1`$. No conclusion about trained predictions follows without that record. Separately, the direct IA projection's single-redshift branch omits the common projection factor that the multi-node branch includes. The emulator assembly applies that factor later. Treat these shell outputs as explicitly defined intermediate quantities, and test the final integration against the multi-node calculation before reusing them in C. [Emulator coordinate](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_emulate_tatt.py#L212), [single-node branch](https://github.com/git-sunao/fastnc/blob/7b2336ce37a909bed51f07f32a2a4f096e71b6ce/fastnc/bispectrum.py#L849).

### Estimator conventions must accompany the model

The interface replaces angular-bin lower edges with an estimated mean separation and subtracts an empirical constant 0.015226 from the conversion factor. Its comment attributes that correction to CosmoGrid. The configuration also applies a HEALPix pixel window when requested. These choices belong to the measurement being reproduced. A DES-Y6 forecast needs its own declared bin averaging, triangle cuts, angular resolution, and pixel treatment. Reusing a simulation-specific conversion without checking the Y6 estimator would preserve a historical code value while changing its physical meaning. [Mean-separation conversion](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_interface.py#L265), [pixel window](https://github.com/rchgomes/3pcf_integrator/blob/d6098d326dcb03bc750adcc9627119f52d928944/python/fastnc_interface.py#L12).

## 6. What this inspection supports

The source supports a staged C implementation that preserves the multipole and FFTLog method, prepares its geometry once, shares IA spectra and triangle invariants across outputs, and contracts directly into the required observables. It also supports retaining the upstream explicit implementation and emulator as separate comparison targets. The proposed C model should reproduce a specified upstream configuration first; changes to nonlinear growth, IA terms, triangle symmetries, tapering, or estimator conventions need their own validation record.

The discrepancies identified here are reasons to define that reference carefully. They are not evidence that every upstream result is affected, and they do not identify the exact commits or private configurations used in either paper. Resolving that provenance with Sunao and Rafael is part of reproduction. The study's architecture and validation chapters describe how to proceed while preserving their scientific and software contributions.
